Week calendar

Seven equal columns, 1px gap on border-subtle so the week reads as one grid. Day minimum height 210px; days in the future take bg-surface-sunken. Day header 10px 12px 8px, weekday 11px/700 uppercase ink-500, date 17px/700, today's date in purple-600. Session block 9px 11px, radius 8px, 8px below the previous. Title 12.5px/700, time and place 11px ink-500, status line 11px/700. Four session states, and only one takes the hatch. A session that has happened with nothing recorded is hatched — it is the lead finding. Partly recorded takes a caution border. Fully recorded is a plain border with its status in ink-500, quiet. A future session takes a dashed purple-200 border: planned, not a gap. The lead card counts sessions that happened and were never written up, with its denominator.

Range and arrangement both use the segmented control

Week/month and calendar/list are the same shape — one of a small set of mutually exclusive presentations of the same data. §4.6a widens to say so rather than inventing a fourth shape.

Recording who came — the grid

Activity header names the session, its time and its place. Beneath it a four-cell tally: invited, attended, did not attend, and not recorded in the hatched cell. Each carries its denominator, and the denominator is the invitation list. An activity nobody wrote up renders 18 hatched rows, never "0 attended". Zero attended says nobody came; nothing recorded says nobody wrote it down. Row 16rem 20rem minmax(0,1fr), gap 20px, padding 12px 24px. Every row carries its own resident identity — avatar, preferred name, full name and room. §2.4 applied per row: on a grid you do not pick the wrong person from a list, you slip a row, and Doris is marked present while Beryl is marked absent. The identity is what stops that. Two answer buttons, equal width, gap 7px, 1.5px border. Did not attend reveals a reason select and cannot be saved without one. An unanswered row shows the hatched pending chip. No row has a default. There is no "mark all attended". One click asserting twelve facts nobody checked is the handover "sign for everybody" failure with twelve people in it. Somebody who joined without being invited is recorded in their own section beneath, on the info tint, and is not added to the invitation list — that would rewrite the plan to say they were always expected. Three facts, all true: 12 of 18 invited attended, 4 not recorded, 2 joined uninvited.

Plan drawer

min(560px,92vw), right-anchored, full height, overlay shadow. Header 20px 24px, title 20px/700. Sections 18px 24px with 11px/700 uppercase headings. Invitation list: each row carries the resident and, beneath, what they have said they like — read from the care plan's social and emotional wellbeing domain, never duplicated into a new field. Where that domain is unwritten the row shows the hatched "never asked what they like", because an unwritten domain is nobody having asked, and the drawer points at the screen that fixes it. A lead note above the list counts how many of the invited have never been asked.
