Measurements — build to these exactly

The goal statement is the largest thing on every screen it appears

16.5px/700 on the tab, 22px/700 on the detail, max 62ch. Every other record in this build is somebody's account of a resident; a goal is the resident's own words about what they want, and it should read like it. "Why it matters" beneath at 13px ink-500, italic, also theirs. Never a clinical paraphrase of either.

Two leads, in order

A resident with no goals at all: the lead is the whole card at 16px/800 and there is no list beneath it — nothing to list. It names how long they have been here, because "no goals set" on somebody admitted yesterday is not a finding and on somebody here eight months is. A resident with goals: the lead is goals past their date with nothing recorded, hatched, with its denominator. A goal with no target date can never be late, and "no date" must never render as "not yet due" — it renders as the hatched tag saying no date was set.

Goal card

minmax(0,1fr) 15rem auto, gap 20px, padding 18px 24px. Linked domain as a purple tag; unlinked as the hatched tag — a goal nobody filed under a domain is a real state and must not default to one. State chip: stalled takes the hatch, moving is plain text with no chip, and the four closures each take their own treatment. Not achieved is not a failure treatment. bg-surface-sunken with a plain border — a fact, not a criticism. Critical and caution are for things somebody must act on; a goal that was not reached is a thing that happened to a person.

What the resident said

Beneath the chip at 11.5px/700. Agreed in positive ink, disagreed in critical ink, not asked in the hatched treatment — it is a gap, and a family reading this later is entitled to know which it was. withdrawn_by_resident carries none, because the withdrawal is the resident's view. Asking what they thought of their own decision is incoherent whichever value it takes.

Progress timeline

6.5rem minmax(0,1fr), gap 18px, padding 14px 0. Its own record, not filtered care notes — so a gap here is a gap in goal progress and makes no claim about the care record. That is why Rule 3c does not apply and a gap marker is honest. A goal open past its target with nothing recorded shows the hatched marker with the duration and what it means: nobody has said, which is not a record that it did not happen. The final row is always when it was set and by whom — the timeline has a beginning, and an empty timeline is a goal that was set and then nothing, not a goal with no history.

Queue

Eighth in the settled shape. Lead is goals past their date with nothing recorded, with its denominator, and the denominator excludes undated goals and says so. Row 13rem minmax(0,1fr) 14rem auto. The statement is the content column, in the resident's words, never truncated to a clinical summary. Sorted by how long past the date.
