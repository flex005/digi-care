Measurements — build to these exactly

The whole module carries Phase 13's constraint

Ordered by name, never by anything a person did. No rankings, no rates, and no counts at all on the staff detail — there is no rota and no shift record, so a count has no honest denominator, and a bare count beside another person's bare count is a ranking the reader performs themselves. Not ordering the list does not prevent that; it only stops us doing it for them. The figures are not wrong, they are wrong without the framing the report gives them. So the detail shows recent activity as a list and links to the report where the period and denominators are stated.

Staff list

Row minmax(0,1fr) 13rem 12rem auto, gap 20px, padding 14px 24px. Avatar 40px, name 14.5px/600, role 12px ink-500 beneath. A line above the table stating the ordering and that it cannot be changed — the constraint said out loud, not merely obeyed. Standing is a union with four members, and only one is quiet. Has access renders as plain text. No longer has access renders settled on bg-surface-sunken with when and why. Suspended takes caution. Never been given access takes the hatch — somebody on the record who was never set up is a gap, not a settled state. Standing carries who changed it and when, always. A standing with no author is a flag, not a record.

Staff detail

Hero 20px 24px, avatar 60px, name 22px/700, role 13px ink-500. Fields three across: role, site, standing. Nothing else. No start date, no contract type, no employment fields — a field nobody has asked for is a field nobody has decided how to protect, and inventing an employment record is how a care system starts holding HR data it was not built to hold. The "Not held here" block sits above the activity, not below it. A page of what somebody recorded reads as the beginning of a performance record unless it is told otherwise first. Reuse the Phase 12 component exactly — solid bg-surface-sunken, plain border, no pattern, because pattern means a gap somebody can close and nothing here can close this one. Activity list 8rem minmax(0,1fr) auto, most recent first, each row linking to the record itself. A note beneath saying the figures live on the report, with their period and denominators, and linking there.

Deactivation

A real act with a confirmation naming the person and stating what does not change: every note, dose and signature they made stays on the record with their name on it. "(deactivated)" is derived from current standing at render time, never snapshotted into the attribution. The snapshot answers how somebody appeared on a record; standing answers whether they can still get in. Only one of those changes, and a reactivated person whose historic notes still said deactivated would be a fact about now stored in a record about then.
