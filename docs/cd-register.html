Discrepancy and never-counted

A discrepancy row takes the critical tint across the whole row; the explanation sits beneath the table, not inside it — 14px 18px, 1px critical border with 4px left. A drug never counted renders no table at all, because there are no entries. It shows the hatched note saying what is missing and what closes it.
