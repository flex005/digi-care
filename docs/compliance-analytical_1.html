There is no overall compliance figure on this screen, and there will not be one. Two of the five Key Questions cannot be rated at all, and no arithmetic turns "amber, unratable, amber, red, unratable" into a percentage that means anything. A single figure would be most reassuring exactly when the record is thinnest — which is now.

What changed, and what did not

Taken from the reference

Varied tile sizes carrying hierarchy. 1.15fr 1fr 1.5fr, gap 18px, stretched to equal height. A dark hero at 52px/800, two stacked minis at 36px/800, a chart panel filling the rest. The layout says what matters before any label does — Rule 5 expressed in geometry rather than type size. A chart where a table was. Stacked bars, recorded against expected-and-missing, so the shape of the record is legible at a glance instead of read out of five rows. A dense table with status dots, hover states, and a filter in the header. Six columns where the old screen had five stacked panels. Radius up to 20px on the outer panels — the reference's softer shell, which suits a screen that is read rather than acted on.

Refused, deliberately

No trend arrows without denominators. The reference's "↑5% than last month" is the exact shape this build spent fifteen phases removing. Where a comparison exists it names both figures. No green hero. The hero is the largest thing on the screen and it carries the count of checks that cannot support a figure — the least reassuring number available, in the most prominent position. No overall compliance percentage. Two Key Questions are unratable, and no arithmetic combines them with three ratings into a figure that means anything. The hatch survives into the chart. Missing evidence is a hatched bar segment, not a lighter shade of the same colour — pattern, not hue, exactly as every other screen. Insufficient Evidence stays a chip, not a colour. In the rating column it renders hatched with its coverage, beside amber and red dots, so the three are never read as a scale.

Where this belongs

Compliance and reports — screens whose job is analysis. Not the operational Dashboard, whose job is what is late right now, and where a chart of the last five months would be the wrong question answered beautifully.
