Centre column carries an arrow AND the word — improved, unchanged, deteriorated. Never the arrow alone: direction by shape is not readable in greyscale and means nothing to a screen reader. Where the level changed, the consequences block renders beneath in the critical treatment, 16px 20px: what changes on the profile, and every post-incident review this closes, named and counted.
