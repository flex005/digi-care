Measurements — build to these exactly

The two screens share machinery and share no figure

The Dashboard is deadline-shaped, over today, ordered by urgency. Compliance is coverage-shaped, over the whole record, ordered by Key Question. No compliance percentage appears anywhere on the Dashboard. A "92% compliant" tile on the front door is the reassurance this product exists to refuse, and it would be most reassuring exactly when the record is thinnest.

Dashboard tiles

Four across, gap 16px, padding 18px 20px. Label 11px/700 uppercase, figure 34px/800, description 12px ink-500 carrying the denominator. Late takes critical with a 5px left border; due-soon takes caution; a tile counting things nobody wrote up takes the hatch. A tile with nothing wrong renders calm — plain surface, ink-700 figure, no green. Nothing on this screen is green. Green would be a reassurance tile, and this screen exists to say what is left. Row 9rem minmax(0,1fr) 13rem auto, gap 18px, padding 12px 24px. Time first at 13px/700 because the screen is ordered by when.

Compliance overview

Five Key Questions in CQC's order, one row each: 14rem 11rem minmax(0,1fr) 11rem auto. Name 17px/700 with the question it asks beneath at 11.5px ink-500. Rating chip 9px 13px, 13px/800. Green, amber and red are ratings; Insufficient Evidence takes the hatch and is not a fourth colour — it is the absence of a finding, not a milder one, and it states its coverage rather than a rating. Coverage bar 8px, split into usable and unusable segments, with the sentence beneath at 11.5px naming both figures. Not held here is its own treatment — solid bg-surface-sunken, 1px border-strong, ink-500, no pattern. Pattern in this system means "a gap you can close", and nothing on any screen can close this one. It is listed, it never counts toward coverage, and it never contributes to a rating. Its wording says the product does not record the thing, never that the home has not done it.

Check rows in the drill-down

minmax(0,1fr) 13rem 11rem. Every check listed whether or not it has data, findings first. Name 14px/600 with the module it comes from beneath at 11px ink-500 — a figure whose origin is not stated is a figure nobody can check. Where the evidence is weaker than it looks, the row says so: fire and legionella are evidenced by a document existing rather than by an assessment record, and the panel says that rather than hiding it behind a tick.

Inspection pack — a manifest, not a file

Three sections in this order: what the pack would contain, what it cannot contain and why, and the plain statement that no file can be produced here. The second section is the useful half and is longer than the first. A manager reading this before an inspection gets more from the gaps than from the list. Two kinds of exclusion, and they render differently: evidence the home has not recorded (hatched — a gap somebody can close) and evidence this product does not hold (bg-surface-sunken — nothing anybody can do here). No download control at all. Not a disabled one — there is no file, and a disabled button implies one could exist.
