Measurements — build to these exactly

Site cards side by side, not a table

1fr 1fr, gap 18px. Two homes read as two homes; a table would read as a league. Head 18px 22px, name 19px/700, counts 12.5px ink-500. A site too small to support most rates carries the hatched tag in its head, once, rather than on every row it affects. Rows minmax(0,1fr) auto, padding 11px 22px. Label 13px with its denominator beneath at 11px ink-500. Figure 14px/800. These are counts, not rates — a count needs no population floor, and per-site counts are what a manager acts on.

Group figures carry their spread in the same sentence as the number

Row minmax(0,1fr) 12rem 12rem, gap 20px, padding 14px 24px. Name 14px/600, then the spread at 11.5px ink-500 naming both homes' figures and any that cannot support one. Each site gets its own column and its own standing. A Key Question that is Insufficient Evidence at one home and Amber at the other renders as exactly that — never as a third figure that is neither. Where a home cannot support a rate its cell is the hatched chip, not a blank and not a zero. Where both cannot, the group has none either: a group figure assembled from two unusable ones is not usable, and it renders as two hatched cells rather than one number.

No group rating, and the screen says so

Inert block at the foot, bg-surface-sunken with a plain border, no pattern — nothing here is a gap somebody can close. It states that judgement belongs on the compliance panel per home, and why a single organisation figure would be the most reassuring thing the product could render.

The cross-site banner

Info tint, 4px left border, 14px 20px. It names the record's home, names the selected one, and says which governs the timestamps — the record's own. It offers the switch and does not perform it. Navigating somebody away from a record they are reading is the app deciding they made a mistake, and a manager covering both homes reads across them all day.
