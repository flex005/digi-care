Measurements — build to these exactly

1 · Domain list

Eighth profile tab. Lead card hatched, 18px 22px, 5px left border, figure 32px/800 — "3 of 10 parts of this person's care have never been written down". Row minmax(0,1fr) 14rem 9rem auto, gap 20px, padding 14px 24px. Domain name 14.5px/600. Beneath it, the first line of the resident's own words at 12px ink-500, italic, truncated to one line — **their voice on the list, not a clinical summary of it**. Where nothing is written the line is absent rather than replaced by a placeholder. State chip 7px 11px, 12px/700 over 11px/400, max 190px: never started takes the hatch, review overdue takes critical, a draft takes info. Due soon and in date are both plain text with no chip — due soon carries its date in caution ink on the second line only. It is a recorded plan approaching a date, the least urgent of the three and the one most likely to crowd the two that matter. Version column 12px ink-500 with the number at 700; blank where never started. Action: "Write this domain" beside the hatch, in its own column after it — separation, not proximity, as the risk list settled. All ten rows always render, iterated from the domain constant, never from the resident's record.

2 · Domain editor

Voice note at the top, 13px 18px, 1px purple-200 with 4px left, purple-50, max 88ch. It states the constraint rather than implying it. Each field 18px 24px, 1px top border. Label 14px/700, guidance 12.5px ink-500 max 70ch. Textarea 104px minimum, 14.5px at line-height 1.6, padding 12px 14px. Placeholders are in the voice the field is written in and italic — first person for current needs and preferences, second person about staff for agreed actions. Where a previous version exists its text renders beneath the field in bg-surface-sunken at 13px ink-500 — visible while writing, never pre-filled into the box. **The editor never pre-fills from an assessment**: a score cannot be turned into "I need help to walk" without putting words in somebody's mouth. An empty field on a draft shows the hatched chip. A draft saves anything; finalising requires all three. Footer: two actions — Save draft, and Finalise and sign. The state line names exactly what is outstanding, and where finalising would close a post-incident review it says which one, by name.

3 · Version history and diff

Row 5rem minmax(0,1fr) 13rem auto, gap 20px, padding 16px 24px. Version number 22px/800 with "version" beneath at 10.5px/700 uppercase. Current version carries a purple pill; it is the only one the Needs tab renders. Summary 13.5px ink-700 naming which fields changed, signature block 11.5px ink-500 with names at 600. **A first finalise is not a diff against nothing** — version 1 renders the hatched note saying there was no previous version, never an empty comparison. Diff: two columns 1fr 1fr, gap 16px. Was on the left in bg-surface-sunken at ink-500; now on the right in positive tint with a positive border. Field label above both at 12px/700 uppercase. An unchanged field renders once, full width, in bg-surface-sunken with "unchanged" as its label — not twice in two columns, which would read as a change that happens to match. Drafts never appear here. A history of intentions is not a history of instructions, and the point of this screen is what staff were told to follow.
