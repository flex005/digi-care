Measurements — build to these exactly

1 · Document viewer

minmax(0,1fr) 21rem, full height, no gap — stage and rail are one surface. Stage background #2A2140: a dark neutral rather than brand purple, so the page it holds reads as paper. Document 640px max width, 6px radius, padding 44px 46px, 0 10px 40px rgba(0,0,0,.35). Zoom top right, page count top left. The sample banner sits above the document, not across it. A watermark would make the sample unreadable and would be the second thing in this build to obscure its own content. It says plainly: representative sample, not this resident's real document, and this build stores no files. Rail carries the metadata the library row shows plus what points at this document — every module referencing it, each linked. That is the broken-reference relationship seen from the other end, and it is what makes the viewer worth opening rather than a picture.

2 · Medication cycle

The honest intake path. A cycle arrives from the pharmacy and the home checks it against what it holds. Nothing here creates a prescription — every row is a change a prescriber already made. Four tallies: new, changed, stopped, and on the MAR but not in the cycle, which takes the hatch. That last one is the finding. A drug the home is still giving that the pharmacy has stopped supplying cannot be seen from either side alone, and it is the reason this screen is a comparison rather than a list. Row minmax(0,1fr) 13rem 16rem auto. A change shows old and new on one line with an arrow, never two rows to compare. Each row is accepted on its own. No "accept all" — one click asserting nineteen medication changes nobody read is the handover failure with prescriptions in it.

3 · Add an interim medication

For what arrives between cycles — a hospital discharge, a GP visit. The source is the first question and it is required. This screen records a prescription that exists elsewhere, and a medication with no source is a drug nobody can trace to a prescriber. Every route leaves a document or a name behind it. A verbal order is the one path with a follow-up obligation: a second member of staff witnesses the call and it must be confirmed in writing within 24 hours. That is real practice and the form carries it. A controlled drug cannot be added by this route at all, and the screen says why rather than failing on submit — a CD needs a written prescription meeting legal requirements, and no interim path substitutes for one. Allergies render beside the resident field, because a prescription is where an allergy is most likely to be checked and most costly to miss. Directions are copied from the source document rather than paraphrased — the MAR chart shows that wording to whoever gives the dose.

What none of these do

No file is stored, no pharmacy is connected, nothing is sent anywhere. Each screen says so where the action is, not afterwards in a toast.
