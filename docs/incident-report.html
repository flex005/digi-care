What you did about it

Measurements — build to these exactly

Page

Content max-width 960px, single column, no wizard and no steps. An incident is reported minutes after it happened by somebody who wants to get back to the resident. Five sections in one card, each padding 20px 24px, 1px bottom border between them. Section heading 12px/700 uppercase letter-spacing .06em ink-500, 14px beneath.

Fields

Label 12px/600 uppercase letter-spacing .04em ink-500, 6px beneath. Inputs padding 10px 12px, 1px border-strong, radius 12px, 14px. Textareas 96px minimum, resizable vertically. Two-up and three-up grids gap 16px; 16px between stacked fields. Hints 12px ink-500 beneath the field, 6px gap.

Subject

Two choice cards, then the picker. Cards padding 12px 14px, 1.5px border, radius 12px, selected takes purple-600 border and purple-50 fill. Title 14px/600, explanation 12px ink-500. Once a resident is chosen, the subject card renders beneath — avatar 42px, name 16px/700, meta 12px ink-500, allergy right-aligned at 11.5px/700 with 4px left border. The allergy is on this screen because a fall and an allergy are read together by whoever picks the incident up. "No resident was involved" is selectable only for equipment failure and near miss, and it is a recorded claim, never a blank.

Severity

Four equal buttons, gap 8px, padding 12px 10px, 1.5px border, radius 12px. Label 13px/700 over a plain-English gloss at 11px/400. Selected takes its own tint and border: positive, info, caution, critical in that order. The words carry the meaning; colour never alone.

Injury and the body map

Three choice cards first — not checked (a gap), checked and none found (a recorded negative), checked and injuries found. The map appears only for the third. Map area grid-template-columns: minmax(0,1fr) 17rem, gap 24px, aligned to start. Front and back side by side, gap 16px, each in a box with 12px padding, 1px border-subtle, bg-surface-sunken, and an 11px/700 uppercase caption. Regions: fill bg-surface with a 1.2px border-strong stroke; hover purple-50 with a purple-400 stroke; marked critical-tint with a 2px critical stroke. Every region is a real button with an accessible name, reachable by keyboard. Left and right are the resident's, not the viewer's — so the resident's left arm appears on the right of the front view and the left of the back view. Every label says which. The marked list sits beside the map, each site a removable row at 13px/600 in the critical treatment. With nothing marked it shows the hatched state, because "injuries found" with no site marked is an incomplete record rather than an empty one.

Footer

Padding 20px 24px. State text left at 13px, naming exactly what is missing. Submit right, 12px 22px, 14px/600, disabled until every required answer is present — including at least one marked site when "injuries found" is chosen.
