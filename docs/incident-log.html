Oldest first — the wait is the finding

Measurements — build to these exactly

This is the fourth queue in the shape /care-notes, omissions and handover already share. Where this does not say otherwise, match those exactly — the point of a fourth is that somebody who has learned one has learned all of them.

Head

Title and "Report an incident" on one row, space-between. The report action is primary and lives here, not in the filter row — it is the only thing on this screen that is not about triage.

Two findings — the one difference from the other queues

grid-template-columns: 1.5fr 1fr, gap 16px, 20px below the head. The lead is the hatched card at 1.5fr: unacknowledged incidents, the thing still fixable by whoever is looking. The secondary is a plain surface card at 1fr: undecided notifications, graver but older, and not what this screen is for. Both carry their denominator and the range. Never summed, never one card — an unacknowledged incident and an undecided notification are different failures and an incident can be both. Figure 32px/800, title 14px/700, description 12.5px max 52ch.

Filters

Pills for status, selects for type and severity — pill means filter, and a twelve-item type list is not a pill row. Padding 14px 24px, gap 8px, 1px bottom border. Default is Not acknowledged, never All. Sort line beneath at 13px ink-500, padding 12px 24px.

Row

grid-template-columns: 15rem 13rem minmax(0,1fr) 9rem auto, gap 20px, padding 14px 24px, 1px top border, aligned to start. The whole row is the button. Resident: name 14px/600 over full name and room 12px ink-500. Where no resident was involved, that is the first column's content in 13px/600 ink-700 with who recorded it beneath — a claim, never an empty column. State chip 7px 11px, 12px/700 over 11px/400, max 200px. Not acknowledged takes the hatch with the wait as its second line. Open takes caution, under review takes info. Closed is plain text with no chip at all — recorded and unremarkable renders quietly. What happened: type 14px/600, then one middot-separated meta line at 11px ink-500 carrying when, where and who reported it. Severity 12px/700 pill, tinted by tier, words carrying the meaning. Beneath it, where something is owed, one line at 11px/700 unrecorded-ink — "notification undecided", "no root cause". This is the only place the secondary finding appears per row, and it is how a reader gets from the second card to the incidents it counts. Action column: one control, "Open".

What must not happen

Do not put a "Acknowledge" button on the row. Acknowledging without reading is the failure the unacknowledged state exists to make visible, and a one-tap control on a list invites exactly that. Acknowledgement happens on the detail screen, after the account has been read.
