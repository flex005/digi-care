Measurements — build to these exactly

Six fields and one question

Content max-width 980px, single column, no wizard. Sections 20px 24px with 12px/700 uppercase headings. Legal name · preferred name · date of birth · admission date · home · room. Nothing else, because anything more asks somebody to guess on the day they know least. Preferred name left blank renders as not recorded rather than defaulting to the legal name — a default here would be the system putting a name in somebody's mouth on their first day. The home field says what choosing it decides: the timezone every clinical record for this person will be written and read in.

Allergies — its own section, in the critical treatment

18px 20px, 5px left border, critical tint. Heading 15px/800. It is the only clinical question on the form and the section says why it is there: the first medication round may happen before anybody asks again. Three states, and the negative carries a source. Recorded with what and the reaction; no known allergies with who said so — the resident, family, a GP letter, a discharge summary; or not known yet, which renders hatched everywhere from the first minute. The source on the negative is what stops the field becoming pressure to answer. "No known allergies" with nobody's name on it is a guess wearing a record, and that is the version that kills somebody. No default selection.

What starts unrecorded — the section that makes the form honest

This section is the phase's argument. Admission does not create a complete record; it creates a person and a set of gaps, and the form saying so is what stops a manager expecting otherwise. Medication is in the list and its destination is "prescriber, not here" — the same distinction as "not held here".
